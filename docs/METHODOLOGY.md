# Methodology

Each route segment maps load and speed to an illustrative raw CO₂ rate. Capture
efficiency is shaped by engine transient load and an ambient-temperature window.
Accepted CO₂ is the minimum of available exhaust CO₂, contactor throughput, and
remaining buffer capacity. Potential capture above those limits is counted as
vented.

The energy proxy combines a regeneration duty reduced by heat recovery and a
vehicle-mass duty. Fuel penalty is converted back to CO₂ using a diesel
combustion factor, allowing gross capture and net avoidance to be reported
separately. Depot offloads reset storage between route batches.
